About

Form first. Phone only.

FixPose exists because the feedback that keeps people safe while training — “drop lower”, “chest up”, “that rep didn’t count” — should not require a trainer standing next to you, or a camera feed leaving your bedroom.

Mission

Why we built it

Most home workouts go wrong in small, invisible ways: a squat that never reaches depth, a push-up with half a range of motion, knees caving in while nobody is watching. Those are exactly the mistakes that a coach corrects in a second — and exactly the ones you cannot see yourself.

FixPose turns the phone you already own into that observer. The camera reads your joints, the state machines decide whether a rep was honest, and a voice tells you what to fix while there is still time to fix it. After the set, everything is written down: reps, cadence, form accuracy, streaks and a report you can export.

What we refuse to do

  • No video uploads. Pose detection runs through Google ML Kit on the handset; camera frames are analysed locally and never sent anywhere.
  • No invented numbers. The features, thresholds and specifications described on this site come from the app itself — we do not publish fake ratings, quotes or statistics.
  • No silent counting. If a rep is dropped or a session is paused, the app says so out loud and on screen instead of quietly adjusting your total.
  • No mystery AI. When the assistant or the planner cannot reach its model, it reports that plainly and falls back to local logic.

How it is built

FixPose is a Flutter application for Android, built around two cooperating engines: a vision side that owns the camera, person lock and landmark smoothing, and a reasoning side that owns the exercise state machines, scores and feedback. Local storage is on-device, statistics can sync to your account, and the AI features are the only parts that talk to the network.
