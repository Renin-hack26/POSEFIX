Feature tour

The whole coaching loop, in one app

Sensing, counting, correcting, planning, eating and reviewing — here is what each part actually does in FixPose v1.1.12.

Live session

Camera view with a real skeleton drawn on top

Start a session and FixPose shows the back-camera preview with the ML Kit pose overlaid on it — every point the model detected, redrawn on each frame, plus live joint angles for the joints that matter to the exercise.

  • Person lock: the app picks one subject and asks others to step out of frame, so a passing friend does not steal your rep count.
  • Framing guide: a shaded region plus “step back / step closer / move left / move right” cues get your whole body in view.
  • Confidence colours: joints are drawn by how sure the model is; weak frames are dropped instead of guessed at.
  • Backgrounded sessions pause and keep their state — come back and you resume where you stopped.

Stylized illustration of the session overlay.

Rep counting

Counting that refuses to cheat

Each exercise has its own state machine with depth and lockout thresholds (a squat must pass the bottom position before it comes back up). A rep is committed only after several confirming frames — so a bounce, a jittery landmark or a two-frame glitch never reaches your total.

  • Full range of motion is required: partial reps are logged as “not counted”, not quietly added.
  • Video guard: point the camera at a phone playing an exercise video and counting pauses with a spoken warning.
  • Too fast / occluded / multi-person states pause counting and tell you why, on screen and out loud.
  • Rejections are spoken with a cooldown — “Too fast — rep not counted”, “Not counted — go through your full range”.

Form score & voice

A score you can hear improving

While you move, the HUD shows a live form score; when the set ends, your sessions store a form accuracy percentage that rolls up into history, home stats and reports. The coach reads the important parts aloud through text-to-speech.

  • Real cue text from the exercise rules: “Go deeper!”, “Keep your chest up!”, “Back knee nearly touching!”.
  • Live angles for the measured joints appear as chips next to the rep counter.
  • Session summary lists total reps, average cadence and form accuracy for the workout.
  • Settings → Spoken cues turns the coaching voice off whenever you want to train in silence.

Training plans

A week you can edit, not just obey

Onboarding asks your level and goal once, then builds a seven-day plan. When the build has an AI key the schedule is generated by the model from the bundled workout library; when it does not — offline, timeout, or a reply that does not follow the contract — a set of local templates is used instead and the plan is honestly labelled as such.

  • Sessions are editable: change the time, status and rounds straight from the Plan tab.
  • Reminders fire daily at the time you pick, and Settings has an on/off switch for them.
  • Next-up slots on Home let you start the scheduled session in one tap.
  • History keeps every session with its form score, duration and reps.

Progress & streaks

The strike: proof you showed up

Finish a session with at least a minute of work and the day is credited. Consecutive days grow the strike, a gap of two days or more resets it — and badges unlock at fixed tiers and are never revoked.

  • Tiers: 3, 7, 14, 30 and 100 days, unlocked once and kept for good.
  • Form-mastery badge: earned by a session at 90% form accuracy or better.
  • Home shows the streak next to a seven-day activity graph and your weekly stats.
  • Longest strike is tracked alongside the current one.

Meal tracking

Calories logged in a couple of taps

Set the daily calorie target you want to hit, then log meals per eating occasion. The Plan tab shows how the day is going against that target without leaving the screen.

  • 100 bundled foods with calories and serving size, plus custom foods you add yourself.
  • Per meal type: breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks are tracked separately.
  • Progress bar on the Plan tab compares today’s intake with your target.
  • VEDA answers calorie questions — “How many calories in 2 eggs + toast?” — with a structured reply.

VEDA AI coach

A chat that knows what you did last

VEDA is the in-app assistant. Every reply is a live model completion grounded in your context — your last sessions, your plan and your numbers — with a firm boundary around medical advice.

  • Context-aware: your five most recent sessions (date, reps, form accuracy) inform the answer.
  • Honest failures: no connection or no key means an explicit “VEDA needs a connection” message — never a faked reply.
  • Offline fallback: AskVEDA can still answer from your own stored data and says so up front.
  • Clear chat is available from the Privacy section in Settings.

Progress reports

Written up, charted and exported as PDF

The Progress report screen assembles your trailing 30 days of training (full history for brand-new accounts) into a readable document, then hands it to the system share sheet as a PDF.

  • Sections: personal details, training summary, body metrics, nutrition summary, recent sessions, analysis & recommendations.
  • Home graph keeps a rolling seven-day view of workout minutes, weekly reps and best form.
  • Suggestions come from the AI when it is reachable, otherwise from deterministic local rules — and the report still generates.
  • Export: PDF with the FixPose header, shared through the Android share sheet.
