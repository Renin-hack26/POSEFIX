What you get

Everything a trainer checks, running on your phone

Camera in, corrections out. FixPose does the sensing, the counting and the nagging so you can just train.

Live skeleton overlay

33 tracked points redrawn on your camera feed every frame, coloured by confidence so you can see exactly what the model sees.

Rep counting you can trust

Per-exercise state machines count only full-range reps, after several confirming frames — partials and camera jitter never make the tally.

Spoken form coaching

“Go deeper!” and “Keep your chest up!” are read out loud mid-set, with a mute switch in Settings whenever you want quiet.

Weekly training plans

Pick a level and a goal once; FixPose builds a seven-day schedule you can edit, with reminders at the time you choose.

Strike streaks & badges

Train on consecutive days to grow your strike. Badge tiers unlock at 3, 7, 14, 30 and 100 days and are never taken away.

Meals & calorie targets

Set a daily kcal goal, log breakfast, lunch, dinner or snacks from a bundled food list — or add your own foods.

VEDA, your AI coach

Chat about your training, your plan or your calories. VEDA answers honestly when it is offline instead of inventing a reply.

Progress reports

Generate a written report of your training, body metrics and nutrition, with suggestions and a PDF you can share.

How it works

Three steps between you and a coached set

No wearables, no tripod app, no upload. Just a phone propped up where it can see you.

Prop up & grant the camera

Open a workout and give FixPose camera access. The framing guide shows where to stand so your whole body fits the frame.

Train — the coach locks on

FixPose locks onto one person, tracks your joints, counts full-range reps and calls out form corrections while you move.

Review the honest summary

Finish and you get total reps, cadence and form accuracy — plus the strike for the day and a slot in your next report.

Under the hood

On-device vision, by design — not as a marketing line

Pose detection runs on the phone through Google ML Kit. Camera frames are processed locally and discarded; nothing about your workout video is uploaded, because there is no upload step.

33

Body points per frame

BlazePose landmarks — face, shoulders, arms, hips, legs and feet — with visibility and confidence for each one.

0

Video frames uploaded

Pose analysis, rep counting and form scoring happen on the handset. The camera stream never reaches a server.

46

Exercise state machines

Bundled movement definitions with their own joint angles, depth thresholds and coaching cues.

Works without a signal

Squat session in a basement or a hostel room: detection, counting and coaching still run. Only AI extras such as plan generation and VEDA chat need a connection.

Your video stays yours

Frames are analysed and dropped on the device. A Settings entry states the on-device processing promise and lets you clear your VEDA chat history.

Anti-cheat counting

Point the camera at a playing video and counting pauses with a warning. Extra people in frame or a hidden body pause it too — and the app says why out loud.
